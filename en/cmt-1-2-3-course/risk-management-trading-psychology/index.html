About the course

Risk Management & Trading Psychology

Most traders don't lose because they lack expertise or technical methods. They lose for two fixable reasons: they have no system to protect their capital, and they cannot control themselves when the market goes against them. The two problems feed each other — a trader with good money management who loses emotional control will still break their own system. Conversely, a trader with a good mindset but no risk rules will do fine until a single trade wipes everything out.

The Risk Management & Trading Psychology course was built to solve both problems. The course is split into 5 sessions — 40% risk management and 60% trading psychology — to build both the tools and the inner foundation of a trader, from basic to advanced. The programme also includes 12 months of guided practice after the course.

The course is now also available as self-paced recorded video, for students with busy schedules who cannot attend the live classes.
