Winning the Game of Stocks is considered essential bedside reading by many beginning stock market investors. The book is clear and easy to understand; the author combines fundamental and technical analysis, but at an accessible level without too much difficult jargon. If you want to learn about this market, it is one of the books you absolutely should not miss.

About the Author of Winning the Game of Stocks

Adam Khoo is a successful entrepreneur, author and one of the world's leading speakers. He went from having nothing to becoming a millionaire at just 26, and he now runs and owns several large businesses with combined annual revenue of up to USD 30 million. He holds a bachelor's degree in business administration from the National University of Singapore (NUS). As a student, he ranked in the top 1% and was selected for the Talent Development Program, which trains the university's most talented and outstanding students.

After graduating, Adam Khoo built businesses in many fields, including advertising, fund management, preschool education, corporate training and learning centers. He is also executive chairman of Adam Khoo Learning Technologies Group – at the time the largest training and education company in Asia. He is also a professional investor and investment adviser to the Wealth Academy FX Growth Fund.

Over his career he has written many popular books, including 12 bestsellers on learning, personal development, parenting and business and finance. All of them made the national bestseller lists and have been translated into 6 languages. Winning the Game of Stocks – is his signature, and best, book on stock trading.

What Winning the Game of Stocks – Adam Khoo Covers

Are you working hard but not building enough wealth to support your life and your dreams, or are your savings not enough for a comfortable retirement? Through Winning the Game of Stocks you will learn how ordinary people, even those with no investing experience at all, built million-dollar fortunes and steady annual incomes.

We live in a volatile economy, so making money is no longer simply a matter of buying good stocks and reliable funds and sitting back while their value grows over time. A volatile, deeply interconnected financial system means that the stocks you own can suddenly plunge one day and bounce back the next, sometimes much higher.

Even so, stocks remain an investment channel that can earn very good returns on your spare money, sometimes even huge ones. That is why becoming a smart investor and mastering effective trading techniques is so important.

Winning the Game of Stocks brings together the author's knowledge and practical investing experience from 21 years in the markets. The strategies in the book help investors stay away from high-risk stocks and pick good companies with strong long-term growth potential.

Some lessons from Winning the Game of Stocks:

  • How to invest in winning stocks that deliver high returns
  • How to tell whether the market is in an uptrend or a downtrend
  • How to avoid risk and protect your portfolio when the market shows signs of collapse
  • How to sell short and profit when the market is in a downtrend
  • How to manage risk and maximize profits
  • How to build a steady mindset when investing
  • How to build a successful portfolio that suits you
  • How to create passive income from real estate investment trusts (REITs)
  • How to build a million-dollar fortune from an average income.

Whether you are just starting out or are already a seasoned investor, this book offers great ideas and superior strategies to grow your income and profits exponentially – the very strategies that helped Adam Khoo build a million-dollar business from nothing.

The strategies in this book helped Adam Khoo build a million-dollar business from nothing.

Our Thoughts on Winning the Game of Stocks

Winning the Game of Stocks is a book every investor should read and reflect on. The author's style is simple, easy to understand and engaging on every page. The book contains many of the secrets that made Adam Khoo one of the youngest self-made millionaires in Singapore at the time. Remarkably, he started with nothing: no inheritance, no bank loans and no outside investment. Reading it closely and understanding it deeply will help you grasp the method that has helped the author, and hundreds of others, achieve outstanding results in life and in stock investing. In short, it is a very good book with great educational value. Winning the Game of Stocks is a top choice for anyone passionate about stocks who is unsure where to begin.
