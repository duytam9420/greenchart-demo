Financial Intelligence is a classic book on corporate finance, offering the best, most detailed and most comprehensive guide to the numbers. Karen Berman and Joe Knight wrote it to be easy to read and understand, so even if you are not a finance expert you can follow the issues they cover clearly and in detail. Financial Intelligence is full of interesting stories about companies. The book also helps non-financial managers confidently understand the mysteries behind the numbers, so they can help their companies grow ever stronger.

Who Are the Authors of “Financial Intelligence”?

Financial Intelligence was written by two well-known finance experts, Karen Berman and Joe Knight:

Dr. Karen Berman is the founder and president of the Business Literacy Institute. She is also a sought-after expert in business and finance. Before founding the Business Literacy Institute, she worked as a consultant and held management roles in banking, investment, healthcare and the graphic arts. She has written many books and articles and frequently speaks nationwide on business and finance.

Joe Knight is the chief financial officer of Setpoint. With his contribution, Setpoint grew strong and was featured in Inc. magazine and in the book A Stake in the Outcome. He is also a partner and senior consultant at the Business Literacy Institute, a well-known speaker and trainer, and the author of many highly regarded books on business and finance.

What Is Financial Intelligence About?

As soon as it was published, Financial Intelligence became a book many businesspeople sought out, because it not only helps readers understand the numbers in financial statements but also explains why they matter. It is a book about mindset that covers the most essential financial information and knowledge, teaching managers how to use financial data to run their companies. The authors also show how to resolve important problems that lead to financial crises because of a lack of basic accounting and finance knowledge.

The book has 7 main parts:

Part 1: The art of finance

Part 2: The peculiarities of the income statement

Part 3: The balance sheet

Part 4: Cash

Part 5: Ratios

Part 6: Applied financial intelligence and the practice of managing working capital

Part 7: Building financially intelligent departments and organizations

Through Financial Intelligence the authors open up a different path and a new vision of how to manage the finances of your own business. The book focuses mainly on core knowledge and the economic issues that create fundamental value, helping managers solve the difficult problems they face in managing and running their businesses.

Notably, the authors also discuss many financial crises to give readers the clearest and most objective picture of the state of the global economy.

Some lessons from Financial Intelligence:

  • Understand the foundations of finance: not only finance but every profession requires basic knowledge. A solid foundation will help you achieve more success in your work.
  • Master the art of finance: the judgment calls behind the numbers are one of the secrets of finance. Within them lie the techniques and keys that set you apart from ordinary businesspeople.
  • Become fluent in financial analysis: once you have the necessary knowledge and techniques, you can easily dig into the numbers behind the art of financial management.
  • Understand the big picture of the economy: the surrounding factors that shape the overall economy are key to understanding customer sentiment and finding your own path within the wider economic context.

Our Thoughts on Financial Intelligence

Financial Intelligence is a truly good and meaningful book that everyone should have on their shelf. It is a book on basic finance, so it is for everyone. Whether or not you work in finance, you should read it at least once; it will give you a clearer view of managing your own finances as well as those of a business. Financial Intelligence will not teach you to work with the numbers in detail like an accountant, but what the authors cover is enough to help you run a business, start one or sharpen your own professional skills. The book distills the extensive research, teaching and speaking experience of two outstanding authors, Karen Berman and Joe Knight, over their careers, so it offers readers enormous value.
