About the course

Learning English the CMTer way

No wandering. Focus on exactly the language that appears in CMT materials and exam questions.

This supplementary module is only for Green Chart's CMT exam prep students. It focuses on English for CMT learning and the exam — it is not an English exam course and does not award a language certificate.

Common obstacles

Technical analysis knowledge isn't always the problem

Many students struggle with the CMT because of specialist English

Reading materials too slowly

It takes a long time to read and process a passage or a question.

Too many technical terms

Lots of unfamiliar finance and technical analysis terms.

Misreading the question

You know the material but may misunderstand what the question asks.

Running out of time

Translating every sentence makes the exam take too long.

Knowing the words but not the sentence

Long English sentences are hard to break down and understand.

Not knowing what to study

Unsure which vocabulary and sentence patterns to focus on.

Learning English the CMTer way

5 parts. 10 sessions.

Scroll to light up the timeline, tap a part to see its content.

PARTS
  • Specialist vocabulary and terminology for each chapter of the CMT curriculum.
  • Common grammar structures in the materials and exam questions.
  • Fast reading methods: finding the main idea and keywords.
  • Real question sets and exercises to apply what you learn straight away.
  • Tips for reading questions and handling them effectively.
Benefits

What will you get from the course?

  • A complete CMT English vocabulary
  • Familiarity with the way CMT materials are written
  • Understanding of common sentence structures
  • Faster reading of the materials
  • Knowing how to find the keywords in a question
  • Handling long English questions more effectively
  • A review method that suits the CMT
  • More confidence when facing the exam
