Q&Aabout the courses.

The questions people often ask about the CMT Level 1, 2 and 3 exam prep courses.

With 12 years of trading and more than 07 years of teaching trading, we have seen that a beginner can absolutely learn to trade and achieve steady profits in the market.

However, besides learning the right knowledge, it takes a serious attitude to practice and a long enough period of experience — at least 06 months to 1 years, and often much longer — depending on how quickly and how seriously each person learns.

The course uses plain language and avoids unnecessary financial jargon, so it suits learners from any background. In fact, our students come from many fields with little connection to finance or economics, such as IT engineers, pharmacists, teachers and TV presenters.

The course is currently only held offline in Hanoi, so students abroad or in other provinces join online. You still interact directly with the lecturer — just in an online classroom instead of a physical one — so the quality of the content and the level of interaction are the same.

After the 08 theory sessions, students are invited to the course's practice group and work alongside the lecturer on real tasks: following daily and weekly market developments, analysing and assessing potential trading opportunities in the current market, practising trades and placing orders, and experiencing trading psychology in the real market.

All Green Chart courses come with free lifetime retakes, so you can sign up again for later classes completely free of charge.

We offer free videos of some parts of the course, so you can get a feel for the content and the lecturer's teaching style here.

The tuition includes the full latest CMT curriculum, a question bank and mock exams written by Green Chart to the CMT programme standard. The course also includes exercise review sessions and mock exam practice once students have finished the theory sessions.

Green Chart is currently the FIRST and ONLY provider in Vietnam offering the complete set of CMT exam prep courses for Level 1-2-3. The CMT Level 2 course has a special tuition fee for students who took the Level 1 course at Green Chart.

The course uses plain language and avoids unnecessary financial jargon, so it suits learners from any background. In fact, our students come from many fields with little connection to finance or economics, such as IT engineers, pharmacists, teachers and TV presenters.

You still interact directly with the lecturer — just in an online classroom instead of a physical one — so the quality of the content and the level of interaction are the same as in an offline class.

The course is taught in Vietnamese, but the content and exam prep materials are presented in English so that students get used to the real exam. The course therefore suits both students who want trading knowledge and those preparing for the exam.

Currently, CMT Level 1 and 2 exam questions are multiple choice, so they only require basic English reading skills (around TOEIC 500); listening and writing skills are not required.

All Green Chart courses have a free retake policy, so you can sign up again for later classes completely free of charge, with no time limit, as long as Green Chart is still running the class.

To retake, simply show that you have registered for (or re-registered for) the next exam with the CMT Association.
