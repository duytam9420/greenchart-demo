Today, the stock market is no longer an unfamiliar investment channel for individual investors. But not everyone can invest effectively and earn high returns. One of the methods professional investors use most to improve their results is learning from books. Lessons from the Greatest Stock Traders of All Time by John Boik is regarded as a stock market classic that every investor should read. The book recounts many feats achieved by great stock traders over more than 100 years. It tells the story of 5 legendary stock traders and the strategies that brought them great success in the stock market.

Lessons from the Greatest Stock Traders of All Time PDF – John Boik

Who Is the Author of Lessons from the Greatest Stock Traders of All Time?

John Boik was born in 1961 and worked as a professional stockbroker before starting his writing career. This book is one of his best-known works; his other titles include How Legendary Traders Made Millions and Monster Stocks, and Lessons From The Greatest Stock Traders Of All Time in particular was named one of the 25 best books of 2004 by Barron's. The author also holds a PhD in biomedical sciences from the University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston. He is president and founder of the social project Principled Societies and of the non-profit cancer research organization New Earth BioMed.

What Is Lessons from the Greatest Stock Traders of All Time About?

The book covers the lives, experiences and investment methods, distilled over years on Wall Street, of some of the most accomplished professional stock traders: Jesse Livermore, Bernard Baruch, Gerald Loeb, Nicolas Darvas and Bill O’Neil. The author portrays each figure's story in detail across different periods. The lessons he draws from these geniuses' successes and failures are an invaluable, practical source of knowledge for investors. At the end of the book, John Boik also brings together all the most popular and successful stock investing methods and strategies.

It turns out that successful investors have specific, detailed rules and techniques. Through this book the author gives readers many effective stock market strategies used by professional investors. He studied the secrets of these stock market geniuses and uncovered many interesting things:

  • Jesse Livermore: his early market failures taught him rule number 1 for making a profit: cut your losses and move on.
  • Bernard Baruch: what skills did Baruch learn from a USD 50-a-week job in the stock market, and how did they help him build a fortune worth millions of dollars?
  • Nicolas Darvas: what did an outsider do to hold his own among the market's top and most successful experts?
  • Gerald Loeb: Loeb recognized the mistakes so many stock investors make, which allowed him to escape the crash of 1929.
  • William O’Neil: he inherited and developed the time-tested rules of earlier professional investors to become a legendary stock investor himself.

Some lessons from the book:

First, skills:

  • Time: investors need to commit time and real effort to the stock market
  • Motivation is needed to reach the goals you set
  • The ability to judge and think, along with humility
  • The ability to adapt yourself to the market
  • Always observe and study the market closely
  • Learn from your own failures
  • Emotional control: force yourself to follow strict, fixed rules.
  • Understand the types of stocks and each of their stages

Second, the principles of an overall strategy for building wealth from stocks

  • Always make decisions based on reason
  • Always take the initiative to research, learn and gain experience
  • Analyze and understand your investment sectors thoroughly
  • Trade as little as possible
  • Research thoroughly and carefully before placing a trade
  • Keep your trades to yourself
  • Always be mentally prepared to step out of the market and wait for a better opportunity

Third, some trading rules

  • Minimize losses and stay in control of risk
  • Identify the market's trend to decide whether to keep trading or stop.
  • Buy high and sell higher
  • Hold on to the hot stocks
  • New stocks lead new markets; don't buy back the old leaders

Our Thoughts on Lessons from the Greatest Stock Traders of All Time

In this book the author paints a detailed picture of the lives of the stock market's great investors. It gives later generations of investors many valuable lessons and insights, recounting the mistakes and stumbles of these great investors and how they overcame difficulties to succeed.

Lessons from the Greatest Stock Traders of All Time is one of the stock investing books that many investors rate highly and recommend. Whether you are just learning about the market, new to investing or already a professional, you should not miss it. It will help shorten your path to success in stock investing. We hope that after reading and studying Lessons from the Greatest Stock Traders of All Time, investors will find their own way to build wealth from stocks and gain more practical investing experience.
