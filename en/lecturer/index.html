Profile

Teaching. Trading. Achievements.

Teaching experience
  • 15 years of trading and 10 years of teaching stock trading and technical analysis, with more than 50 courses delivered successfully.
  • One of the first Vietnamese to hold the CMT Charterholder designation, awarded by the CMT Association (USA).
  • One of the first people to teach the CMT designation in Vietnam.
  • Has taught many investment analysis courses at leading universities such as the Foreign Trade University, the National Economics University and the Academy of Policy and Development.
  • Expert speaker and judge at many major programmes and competitions, including VTV1's finance and business news, VTV Khớp lệnh, Vietnam and the Indices, the Go Finance competition and the ASC talent final.
Professional experience
  • Market Strategy Specialist – VPS Securities JSC.
  • Head of Market Strategy – AIMS Futures Vietnam (trading member of the Mercantile Exchange of Vietnam).
  • Senior FX Trader – MSB bank.
  • Many years as a trader, stock analyst and in similar roles at banks and brokerage firms.
Professional achievements
  • CMT Charterholder.
  • Certificate of merit from the Chairman of the State Securities Commission of Vietnam, 2015.
